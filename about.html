Our Story

Passion for sneakers. Commitment to authenticity.

More Than Just Shoes

Timmy Kicks was founded with one goal: to make premium sneakers and sportswear easily accessible to customers who value quality, comfort and style.

From Nike and Air Jordan to Adidas, Vans and Dr. Martens, we carefully select every product to ensure authenticity and long-lasting quality.

Whether you're looking for everyday sneakers, limited editions or football jerseys, Timmy Kicks delivers products you can trust.

To provide authentic sneakers, premium jerseys and quality streetwear at affordable prices while delivering excellent customer service and a seamless shopping experience across Kenya.

Our Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted sneaker and sportswear store by offering genuine products, innovative shopping experiences and unmatched customer satisfaction.
